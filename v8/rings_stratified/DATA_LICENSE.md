# Rings-count data-source boundary

These structure-free aggregate tables derive from the frozen B-XAIC rings-count task. B-XAIC is reported by its dataset card as CC BY-SA 4.0 and is attributed in `DATA_SOURCES.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

No raw structures, molecule or source-row identifiers, per-molecule predictions or attributions, checkpoints, or score caches are included. The repository's MIT License applies to repository-authored code and documentation, not to the upstream dataset.
